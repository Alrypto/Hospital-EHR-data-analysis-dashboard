# Hospital-EHR-data-analysis-dashboard
# Project Overview

This project analyzes **5,000 hospital patient records** to understand patient demographics, clinical outcomes, hospital operations, and financial performance.

The goal was to transform hospital Electronic Health Record (EHR) data into meaningful insights that could help hospital management make better decisions around **staffing, patient care, resource allocation, health insurance, and revenue management.**

I built the project entirely in **Microsoft Excel,** using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, calculated measures, and dashboard design techniques.

# Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key healthcare and business questions:

- Which departments receive the most patients?
- Which departments generate the most revenue?
- What are the most frequent diagnoses?
- What are the patient outcomes?
- What percentage of patients use health insurance?
- Which cities contribute the most patients?
- What is the average patient length of stay?
- Which blood groups and genotypes are most common?
- Which age groups have the most patients?
- Which gender has more hospital visits?
- Does length of stay affect hospital billing?
- Which departments have high patient volume but lower revenue per patient?
- Which diagnoses require more hospital resources?
- Which patient groups have higher referral rates?

# Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

- The hospital recorded 5,000 patients in the analyzed dataset.
- Total recorded revenue was approximately ₦245.23 million.
- The average patient bill was approximately ₦49.05K.
- The overall recovery rate was 33.6%.
- The average hospital stay was approximately 5.5 days.
- The average patient age was 48 years.
- Emergency recorded the highest departmental revenue at approximately ₦59.15M.
- Diabetes and Hypertension were among the diagnoses generating substantial hospital revenue.
- Patients using NHIS generated approximately ₦83.18M, while patients without insurance generated approximately ₦83.83M.
- Lagos recorded the highest patient volume among the cities analyzed.

**Note:** These insights are based on the dataset used for this portfolio project and should not be interpreted as actual hospital statistics.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I developed the following recommendations:

1. # Increase Healthcare Staffing

More healthcare workers should be considered in departments with high patient volumes to reduce workload, improve patient flow, and potentially reduce patient length of stay.

2. # Strengthen Care for Older Patients

Targeted monitoring and preventive-care programs should be considered for older patients, particularly for health conditions that are more common within older age groups.

3. # Expand Community Health Awareness

The hospital should increase community health education around genotype, blood groups, diabetes, hypertension, and other common health conditions.

4. # Promote Health Insurance Enrollment

More awareness should be created around the importance of health insurance and how eligible individuals can enroll.

5. # Strengthen Preventive Healthcare

Community education on healthy lifestyles, safety practices, disease prevention, and early medical intervention could help reduce avoidable emergency visits.
