# Hospital-EHR-data-analysis-dashboard
# Project Overview

This project analyzes **5,000 hospital patient records** to understand patient demographics, clinical outcomes, hospital operations, and financial performance.

The goal was to transform hospital Electronic Health Record (EHR) data into meaningful insights that could help hospital management make better decisions around **staffing, patient care, resource allocation, health insurance, and revenue management.**

I built the project entirely in **Microsoft Excel,** using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, calculated measures, and dashboard design techniques.

# Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key healthcare and business questions:

- Which departments receive the most patients?
- Which departments generate the most revenue?
- What are the most frequent diagnoses?
- What are the patient outcomes?
- What percentage of patients use health insurance?
- Which cities contribute the most patients?
- What is the average patient length of stay?
- Which blood groups and genotypes are most common?
- Which age groups have the most patients?
- Which gender has more hospital visits?
- Does length of stay affect hospital billing?
- Which departments have high patient volume but lower revenue per patient?
- Which diagnoses require more hospital resources?
- Which patient groups have higher referral rates?

# Dashboards

The project contains 4 interactive dashboards.

1.  # Hospital Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of hospital performance.

## Key KPIs:

- Total Patients
- Total Revenue
- Average Bill
- Recovery Rate
- Average Length of Stay
- Average Patient Age

2.  # Demographics & Patient Profile

Analyzes the characteristics of patients using the hospital.

## Analysis includes:

- Patients by Age Group
- Visits by Gender
- Blood Group Distribution
- Genotype Distribution
- Patients by City
- Blood Group × Genotype

3.  # Clinical & Operational Performance

Examines the hospital's clinical activity and operational workload.

## Analysis includes:

- Most Frequent Diagnoses
- Patient Outcomes
- Patient Volume by Department
- Average Length of Stay
- Referral Patterns
- Monthly Patient Admissions

 4.  # Financial Performance

Analyzes hospital revenue and payment behavior.

## Analysis includes:

- Monthly Revenue
- Revenue by Department
- Average Bill by Department
- Revenue by Payment Method
- Insurance vs Self-Pay
- Revenue by Diagnosis
